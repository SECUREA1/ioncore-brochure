Solution profiles

Purpose-built battery platforms engineered for megawatt performance.

Every configuration below is optimized for 1.2 MWh delivery with distinct thermal management, electronics, and labor strategies. Configure to your site requirements and scale production with predictable economics.

Custom Prismatic Cell Platform

High-density, precision-cooled pack for mission-critical infrastructure.

  • 4,002 prismatic cells at 300 Wh each across ten monitored modules.
  • Copper plate liquid loop with redundant pumps and thermal sensing.
  • Smart inverter suite with MPPT optimization and predictive maintenance.
  • $350K material cost, $45K labor — 45% gross margin at $575K retail.
  • Ideal for data centers, EV corridors, and industrial microgrids.

Soup Can Cylindrical Array

Accessible, modular bins designed for rapid field replacement and education markets.

  • 60 stackable bins holding 200 cylindrical cells (100 Wh) each.
  • Passive airflow chassis with directed fan assists and thermal pads.
  • Simplified inverter and manual-fuse BMS with intuitive status LEDs.
  • $238K materials, $25K labor — 35% margin at $308K market price.
  • Best fit for agricultural, rural microgrid, and DIY solar programs.

Hybrid FlexPack System

Blends prismatic base load with cylindrical peak support for ultimate resilience.

  • 2,000 high-density prismatic + 6,000 cylindrical cells orchestrated by AI.
  • Dual-stage cooling with cross-linked heat exchangers.
  • Dual MPPT controllers and failover inverter clusters.
  • $270K material stack, $40K labor — 50% margin at $400K retail.
  • Tailored for smart grids, EV depots, and mobile disaster relief fleets.

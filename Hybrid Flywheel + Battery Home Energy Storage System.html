System overview

Why hybrid? Energy is stored as rotational power inside a high-speed flywheel for instantaneous response and within advanced lithium batteries for long-duration supply.

Result: Whole-home coverage that handles daily consumption, peak surges, and outage protection with no single point of failure.

  • Grid backup, smoothing, and peak shaving.
  • Confidently starts HVAC compressors, pumps, and other high-surge loads.
  • Designed for garages, basements, or dedicated energy rooms.

Main advantages

Rapid surge response

Flywheel instantly covers motor and compressor startup loads so the battery experiences minimal stress.

Long-duration backup

The lithium pack provides overnight or multi-hour coverage, maintaining critical circuits through grid outages.

Built-in resilience

Dual storage paths, safety braking, and real-time monitoring safeguard against battery or grid faults.

Scale your system.

Product description

The IonCore FiberCut M1 Pro is a premium industrial-grade mobile cutting system that separates heavy power conversion, thermal rejection, and process support from the moving tool platform. A low-profile tracked base carries the cutting head, precision Z-axis, standoff sensing, and motion control, while an offboard power and process module manages stabilized DC distribution, hot-swappable energy storage, supercapacitor buffering, liquid cooling, assist gas regulation, and system orchestration.

  • Industrial body architecture: sealed panel design, protected cable routing, reinforced tracker frame, low-center-of-gravity stance.
  • Premium cut stability: power-to-motion coupling, autofocus correction, tracked feed-rate regulation, standoff-gated emission.
  • Field-ready uptime: modular service bays, quick-swap optics consumables, serviceable cooling drawer, hot-swappable energy cartridges.
  • High-value product positioning: engineered as an upgradeable direct-energy platform with optional premium packages and enterprise features.

System architecture and modules

The M1 Pro is divided into premium serviceable modules. Each major subsystem is built to be maintainable, upgradable, and independently validated, reducing downtime and improving enterprise readiness.

1. FiberCut M1 Pro Head

Industrial head assembly with stabilized collimator chamber, fast autofocus, quick-swap protective window, contamination sensing, back-reflection monitoring, purge management, and a premium-machined nozzle block.

  • 1 kW fiber-coupled optical path
  • Autofocus travel target: ±10 mm
  • Quick-swap window cassette for field service
  • Optional kerf camera and nozzle identification

2. Precision Tracker Platform

Low-profile tracked robotic base with widened stance, enclosed drive housings, internal routing paths, IMU-based motion awareness, and active Z-axis height management coordinated with standoff sensing.

  • Closed-loop speed regulation
  • Dual-rail Z-stage with anti-backlash design
  • Shock-isolated head mount and upper deck stiffening
  • Surface-following cutting support

3. Offboard Power & Process Module

Premium field power module with locking caster base, cartridge bay, supercap drawer, control core, labeled quick-connect panel, gas regulation zone, and industrial service access architecture.

  • Hot-swappable LFP battery magazines
  • Precharge + contactor bus engagement
  • High-capacitance supercapacitor stabilization
  • Telemetry, diagnostics, and event logging

4. Gen 2 Cooling Architecture

Premium dual-loop thermal system with dedicated head loop, separate electronics loop, shared heat rejection, serviceable radiator access, and optional Cryo Lite sub-ambient optical stabilization.

  • Microchannel head cooling
  • Window thermal ring for optics stability
  • Cooling drawer service module
  • Adaptive dew-point and purge logic

5. Industrial Control & Interface Layer

Integrated control environment supporting safety interlocks, process gating, recipe storage, live telemetry, service logs, material presets, and future fleet-ready diagnostics.

  • Ready-to-cut workflow guidance
  • Fault timeline and maintenance alerts
  • Optional 5–7 inch industrial touchscreen
  • Remote diagnostics-ready architecture

6. Umbilical & Process Delivery System

Protected side-routed umbilical carrying fiber delivery, stabilized DC power, coolant supply/return, assist gas, and communications while preserving tracker mobility and bend-radius safety.

  • Dry-break quick-connect cooling couplers
  • Keyed interfaces and strain relief
  • Protected fiber routing geometry
  • Field-service bulkhead access

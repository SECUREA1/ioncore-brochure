How Omconsole Technology Works
Each project is tuned, but the flow is simple: sense, fuse, map, and move.
Step 01
Capture Signals
Connect EEG, EMG, cameras, microphones, and site sensors into Omconsole Core through the MindLink, MuscleLink, GestureDeck, and ToneGate modules.
Step 02
Fuse & Tune
Use tuners and dashboards to set thresholds, modes, dead-zones, and safety logic. Signals are fused into a single Omconsole control profile.
Step 03
Map to Robotics
Route Omconsole outputs into RoboBridge I/O or your existing control stack. Map gestures, muscle events, and tones to movements, tasks, or macros.
Step 04
Operate & Learn
Run the system, log behavior, and refine. Omconsole is built to plug into your AI models, task managers, and learning pipelines.

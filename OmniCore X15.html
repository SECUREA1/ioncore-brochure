Core technologies powering OmniCore X15

Every subsystem is orchestrated for high-availability microgrid support, remote deployments and mission-critical operations.

Magnetic inertia generation

  • High-efficiency rotor assembly with hybrid ceramic bearings.
  • Dynamic torque balancing eliminates vibration and power ripple.
  • Optimized for sustained 8 kW output with 12 kW surge handling.

Thermal stewardship

  • Industrial fan array regulates airflow across the generator stack.
  • Cryo-ready manifolds accept liquid nitrogen modules for ultralow temps.
  • Intelligent fail-safes prevent thermal runaway and extend component life.

Energy storage orchestration

  • 30 kWh lithium bank with modular expansion to 50 kWh.
  • Integrated inverter harmonizes AC and DC output channels.
  • Advanced BMS safeguards voltage, temperature and charge cycles.

Operational intelligence

  • OLED command interface with companion iOS and Android app.
  • Edge analytics monitors load trends and dispatches predictive alerts.
  • Ready for SCADA, solar and wind integrations via secure API.

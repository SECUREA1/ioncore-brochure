Rapid deployment highlights

  • No excitation source required for start-up or regulation.
  • Direct-drive compatible to remove gearbox losses.
  • Active thermal management for steady-state performance.
  • Integrated health monitoring with predictive analytics.

High-efficiency powertrain

Permanent magnet rotors and low-loss laminations deliver immediate torque response with minimal electrical resistance, ensuring rapid synchronization and superior power quality.

  • High copper fill factors for optimized flux linkage.
  • Precision-balanced rotors for vibration-free operation.
  • Dynamic voltage regulation across load profiles.

Modular architecture

Segmented stator assemblies and configurable pole counts enable rapid customization for wind, hydro, EV regeneration, or hybrid storage integration.

  • Compact footprint for nacelle or containerized installations.
  • Scalable voltage classes from 400 V to 13.8 kV.
  • Hot-swappable subassemblies minimize downtime.

Built-in resilience

Corrosion-resistant alloys, encapsulated windings, and redundant sensing provide reliable uptime under marine spray, desert heat, or polar cold.

  • IP67 sealing with conformal coating protection.
  • Multi-axis vibration monitoring and fault alerts.
  • IEC 61400-1 compliant for global certification pathways.

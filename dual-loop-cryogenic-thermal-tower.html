Dual-Loop Cryogenic Thermal Tower

A mid-size magnetic inertia unit engineered to stabilize high-density power systems with Ioncore-grade cryogenic control. Dual-loop coolant routing and adaptive thermal mapping keep inertia stacks operating at peak efficiency.

  • Dual-loop coolant routing with independent flow modulation for redundancy and precision.
  • Magnetic inertia core stabilization tuned for mid-size deployment footprints.
  • Vertical thermal tower layout for rapid heat extraction and serviceable access points.

Loop A — Primary Cryogenic Spine

Ioncore’s primary loop maintains the core cryo jacket, prioritizing low-noise circulation and peak-load extraction. Built-in flow throttles keep the inertia rotor within its optimal magnetic envelope.

Loop B — Stabilization + Recovery

The secondary loop provides rapid recovery during surge scenarios, rerouting thermal payloads into the tower’s upper exchanger array for immediate dissipation and controlled return.

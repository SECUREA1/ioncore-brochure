How the FSCU keeps energy in motion

The Flywheel Self-Charging Unit (FSCU) captures rotational inertia inside a low-drag vacuum shell, converting every watt into responsive, cable-free power.

1 • Spin

Carbon-composite flywheel levitated on active magnetic bearings accelerates within a sealed vacuum housing to minimize friction losses.

2 • Store

A graphene supercapacitor buffer and Power Control PCB orchestrate RPM, temperature, and balance, banking kinetic energy with millisecond telemetry.

3 • Serve

Bidirectional inverter converts momentum into on-demand output via Qi 2.0 magnetic wireless pads or USB‑C PD 3.1 while smoothing loads on the grid.

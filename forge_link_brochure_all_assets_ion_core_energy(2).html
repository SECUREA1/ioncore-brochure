Market Context & Positioning

ForgeLink addresses the growing demand for portable, high‑precision manufacturing across field robotics, repair depots, and advanced prototyping labs. Competing solutions are either stationary CNC lasers or low‑power handhelds with limited duty cycles. ForgeLink’s differentiation is a modular Yb‑fiber platform that scales from wearable to table‑size stations while maintaining Class‑4 safety, AI‑assisted controls, and energy‑dense power buffering. This enables on‑site fabrication where uptime and mission continuity matter.

  • Primary segments: defense & field service robotics, heavy industry maintenance, remote infrastructure, aerospace labs.
  • Value props: rapid deployment, autonomous operation, premium beam quality (M² < 1.2), and reduced downtime via integrated diagnostics.
  • Go‑to‑market: direct enterprise pilots, certified integrators for Omconsole, and limited Founders editions for early adopters.

Technical Brief (NDA Excerpt)

ForgeLink Overview

The Omconsole ForgeLink Tool Attachment is a high‑precision, AI‑integrated direct‑energy system for autonomous fabrication. It enables on‑demand thermal generation, metal cutting/welding, and structural manipulation in confined or remote environments. Core energy buffering uses a flywheel + Li‑ion pack (2s3p, ~222 Wh) with PMSG harvesting and Class‑4 laser safety interlocks.

  • Laser: Yb‑fiber, 1030–1070 nm, 1–5 kW (model dependent)
  • Beam Quality: M² < 1.2; spot ~100 µm with adaptive focus
  • Cooling: Dual‑loop liquid with optional cryogenic assist
  • Controls: STM32/ESP32, PID thermal loop, AI material classification

Subsystems Snapshot

  • Energy: SS304 rotor, NdFeB 8‑pole array, M19 stator, active rectification
  • Sensing: Hall ×4, optical encoder, MLX90640 thermal grid
  • I/O: HMI touchscreen, CAN bus, UART diagnostics, e‑stop & interlocks
  • Ingress: IP54; operating −10°C to 65°C with active cooling

Ytterbium Fiber Emitter — Key Specifications

Core Capability

  • Precision cutting/welding on steel, aluminum, titanium
  • High wall‑plug efficiency (>40%) for portable duty
  • Fast modulation to ~50 kHz for dynamic heat control

Typical Ranges

  • Power: 200 W – 1.5 kW (CW); higher via rack modules
  • Wavelength: 1030–1090 nm (nominal 1070 nm)
  • Form factor: modular, rack or backpack integration

Beam Quality

  • M² < 1.1–1.2 (near diffraction limited)
  • Spot ~100 µm with autofocus head
  • Assist gas ready (N₂/O₂/Ar) for clean edges

Compliance & Safety

ForgeLink systems are Class‑4 laser devices. Operation requires certified training, protective eyewear (OD≥7 at 1030–1070 nm), interlocked enclosures, and local compliance (e.g., ANSI Z136, IEC 60825‑1). Export controls may apply.

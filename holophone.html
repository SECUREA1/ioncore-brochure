Holophone experience

FAQ

Answers for launch partners.

Holophone™ was engineered for enterprise storytelling and premium consumer experiences. Here are the questions we hear most.

Is data on the Power Coin private?

Yes. Storage is hardware-encrypted and only mounts after a biometric handshake with your Holophone.

Can I operate multiple coins?

Absolutely. Swap coins throughout the day or dedicate modules for work, media, and travel. The phone stays live via the supercap buffer.

Does the system work offline?

Yes. AR assets, tickets, and payment tokens remain on-coin for offline use, then sync when connectivity returns.

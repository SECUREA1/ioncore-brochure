Technology

Inside the Ioncore magnetic inertia platform

Each portable generator is engineered as a dual-mode energy system—storing kinetic energy and returning it as precise, clean electricity on demand.

Magnetic bearing stack

Frictionless ceramic bearings with N52 preload magnets eliminate mechanical wear while maintaining micron-level rotor stability.

Dual-mode stator

M19 laminated cores and 1,000-turn windings transition instantly between motor and generator modes for seamless charge/discharge cycles.

Liquid micro-cooling

Closed-loop micro pump, microchannels, and radiator assembly keep stator temperatures below 60 °C under peak load.

Integrated inverter

Onboard DC-DC boost with 100 W pure-sine inverter and USB-PD delivers flexible AC and high-speed DC outputs.

Controls (keyboard + gesture)

  • WASD thrusters. Shift boost.
  • R/F depth up/down. Mouse (or right-hand gesture) aims heading.
  • Space / Click fires. E sonar scan.
  • Gesture Mode: Press G or tap “Gesture” to turn on. Use pads to move/aim + pinch actions.
  • T opens tuners. L toggles log. P pause.

Tip: Use tuners to adjust Scan Hold Time, Input Smoothing, and Thrust Multiplier.

Mission

  • Scan ruins, vents, and sample nodes.
  • Collect samples by hovering near nodes.
  • Survive hostiles: shoot to protect the hull.

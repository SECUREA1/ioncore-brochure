Signature innovations

  • Frictionless 20 mm rotor suspended by hybrid ceramic and active magnetic bearings.
  • Four N52 neodymium arrays and 500-turn copper coils deliver whisper-quiet power.
  • Smart power conditioning and LM2596 regulation stabilize 5 V delivery for lighting and lifestyle accessories.
Engineering mastery

Precision architecture crafted for independence.

Every MagnaLuxe™ console merges kinetic art with resilient energy hardware—ensuring the spectacle of motion is matched by dependable, concierge-grade performance.

Magnetic drive assembly

  • 20 mm stainless-steel shaft balanced for up to 3,000 RPM.
  • Active magnetic and hybrid ceramic bearings maintain frictionless rotation.
  • Liquid cooling blocks protect the rotor under continuous showcase operation.

Electromagnetic core

  • Four N52 rare-earth magnets (10 × 5 mm, 1.4 T) paired to a silicon-steel stator.
  • 500-turn copper coil optimised for responsive, low-noise generation.
  • Armoured enclosure isolates the core for safety and elevated aesthetics.

Power intelligence

  • ARM microcontroller orchestrates rectification, regulation, and monitoring.
  • Sensor suite tracks voltage, current, and thermal load in real time.
  • LM2596 buck converter conditions a stable 5 V DC output for lighting and USB accessories.

Energy reserve

  • 3.7 V / 2000 mAh Li-ion cell with full overcharge and discharge protection.
  • Delivers 2.5 hours of uninterrupted 3 W illumination.
  • Ready to power companion devices such as personal electronics or concierge displays.

System Architecture

Precision-engineered assemblies for seamless energy orchestration.

Each subsystem is crafted to minimize drag, stabilize thermal loads, and preserve stored energy so every installation performs like a concierge-managed power plant.

Magnetic Bearing Stack

Levitation bearings suspend the rotor assembly, removing mechanical contact and enabling ultra-high rotational speeds.

  • Active levitation feedback maintains micrometer precision.
  • Zero-contact rotation boosts lifespan and efficiency.
  • Flux containment preserves structural integrity.

Electromagnetic Drive

Tri-phase electromagnets orchestrate acceleration, braking, and torque output, ensuring smooth response at any load.

  • Adaptive pulse sequencing limits power loss.
  • Dynamic torque control matches demand curves.
  • Safety interlocks secure operation in mission-critical zones.

Intelligent Control Unit

Sensor-rich control core supervises thermal conditions, vibration, and storage balance with AI-driven decisioning.

  • Embedded diagnostics predict maintenance windows.
  • Continuous telemetry informs fleet operators.
  • Secure firmware defends against tampering.

Thermal & Storage Core

Hybrid cooling keeps coils and batteries in optimal ranges while modular storage expands from handheld packs to 200 kWh banks.

  • Cryogenic integration for high-density sites.
  • Lithium-ion suites scale per application.
  • Active airflow directs heat away from sensitive zones.

Technology Stack

Hybrid power intelligence engineered for uptime.

Peak Series modules orchestrate kinetic storage, lithium reserves, and advanced firmware to keep your equipment energized without voltage sag or noise.

Energy Flow

  • Charge: DC bus boosts to 24 V and spins the flywheel in motor mode.
  • Store: Kinetic stage captures 6–7 Wh while lithium pack secures 74 Wh.
  • Discharge: Generator mode recovers kinetic energy to refill the DC rail.
  • Deliver: Clean DC backbone or conditioned 120 VAC on Peak 50.

Control & Telemetry

  • Arduino Nano supervises charge states, power delivery, and safety interlocks.
  • Hall-effect sensors provide real-time RPM and kinetic reserve awareness.
  • Firmware-tunable profiles support research, broadcast, and tactical scenarios.

Thermal & Service

  • Liquid micro-loop stabilizes the flywheel core under sustained draw.
  • Quick-access panels simplify module swaps and accessory integration.

Signal Intelligence Suite

Tools that power Startup Radio fieldwork

Ioncore engineers operate two complementary applications to monitor, validate, and map the wireless spectrum around every broadcast site.

Ioncore MAC Intelligence — Address Orchestration

ioncore_mac_intelligence.py is a lightweight MAC and Bluetooth Low Energy inspector that normalizes device lists, flags multicast versus unicast traffic, and surfaces Bluetooth address types in seconds.

  • Import CSV spreadsheets or paste raw captures directly.
  • Auto-identify vendor OUIs and note locally generated addresses.
  • Cross-reference local ARP/NDP caches to enrich IP and interface context.
  • Export audit-ready CSV or JSON summaries for compliance teams.

Spectrum Command Center — Multi-Radio Environment Manager

spectrum_command_center.py is an asynchronous, multi-threaded command center that fuses Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular telemetry with GPS positioning to visualize our live deployment theatre.

  • Combines BLE scans, classic Bluetooth discovery, Wi-Fi, and cell-ID sweeps.
  • Calculates distance with tunable path-loss propagation models and quality grading.
  • Plots devices onto interactive 2D maps and 3D terrain views for mission planning.
  • Maintains an SQLite knowledge base with custom device labels and history.

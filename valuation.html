Readiness checklist

Key steps to move from lab validation to pilot-grade deployments.

  • Verification & validation
    • Structured test matrices for storage (UL 9540/9540A, IEEE 1547.1) and propulsion (DO-160/DO-178/DO-254 equivalents).
    • Accredited lab testing for efficiency, safety, and endurance.
  • Certification pathway
    • Map sector requirements (UL/CE/CSA for storage; FAA/EASA for propulsion) with timelines and responsible owners.
    • Export-control review (ITAR/EAR) for defense-adjacent propulsion modules.
  • Quality & supply chain
    • QMS aligned to ISO 9001/AS9100; supplier qualification for superconducting materials and precision machining.
    • Reliability engineering (HALT/HASS) and documented MTBF/MTTR expectations.
  • Cyber & control hardening
    • IEC 62443/ISO 27001 baselines, secure OTA updates, audit logging, and safety interlocks for connected dashboards.
